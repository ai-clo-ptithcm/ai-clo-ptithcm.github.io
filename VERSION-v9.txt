AI-CLO PTITHCM v9 — Complete Assessment Flow
Base: ai-clo-ptithcm.github.io-ngay28-8(1).zip
Date: 2026-08-28

Core flow:
1) Teacher configures assessment by chapter/topic/CLO counts.
2) Questions are sampled from active question bank and frozen in exam_questions.
3) Student starts/resumes timed attempt with autosave.
4) Supabase RPC scores answers and returns total/CLO/chapter results.
5) Teacher views student/class dashboards and exports reports.
6) Gemini is strictly on-demand and cached; no automatic AI call after submission.
